WhatsApp Translate
==================

A WhatsApp client for your Mac that translates your chats automatically.

Setup (once)
------------
1. Move "WhatsApp Translate.app" to your Applications folder (optional).

2. Right-click "Setup Codex.command" and choose Open, then click Open again.
   - It installs the Codex CLI if you don't have it, then opens your browser
     so you can sign in with your ChatGPT account. The window closes by itself
     when everything is ready.
   - If macOS says it "can't verify" the file: open System Settings →
     Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway", and try again.
     (This happens because the app isn't from the App Store.)

3. Open WhatsApp Translate (right-click → Open the first time) and scan the QR
   code with your phone: WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device.

Using it
--------
- Opening a chat turns translation on: messages you receive get an Italian
  translation underneath, and what you type is translated into French.
  Change the model and the languages per chat in the bar at the top.
- Press Enter to see the translation of your message, then click
  "Send translation" (you can edit it first), or "Send original".
- Your chats, photos and files are stored only on your Mac.
- Closing the window keeps the app running in the background. Quit with ⌘Q.

Good to know
------------
- Translation uses your own ChatGPT/Codex account and its usage limits.
- This is an unofficial WhatsApp client, so use it at your own risk.

WhatsApp Translate
==================

A WhatsApp client for your Mac that translates your chats automatically.

Setup (once)
------------
1. (Optional) Drag "WhatsApp Translate.app" into your Applications folder.

2. Double-click "Setup Codex.command".
   macOS will block it the first time, saying it can't verify the file,
   because it doesn't come from the App Store. To allow it:
     a. Click "Done" in that message.
     b. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to
        "Setup Codex.command was blocked", and click "Open Anyway".
     c. Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" once more.
   You only need to do this once.

   The setup then runs in a Terminal window:
   - it installs the Codex CLI if you don't have it;
   - it opens your browser so you can sign in with your ChatGPT account;
   - it unblocks the app, opens it, and closes the window.

3. In WhatsApp Translate, scan the QR code with your phone:
   WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device.

Using it
--------
- Opening a chat turns translation on: messages you receive get an Italian
  translation underneath, and what you type is translated into French.
  Change the model and the languages per chat in the bar at the top.
- Press Enter to see the translation of your message, then click
  "Send translation" (you can edit it first), or "Send original".
- Your chats, photos and files are stored only on your Mac.
- Closing the window keeps the app running in the background. Quit with ⌘Q.

Good to know
------------
- Translation uses your own ChatGPT/Codex account and its usage limits.
- This is an unofficial WhatsApp client, so use it at your own risk.
